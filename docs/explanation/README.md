# Explanation

**Understanding-oriented documentation**

## Purpose

Explanation provides context, background, and deeper understanding. It is **understanding-oriented**, helping readers comprehend the "why" behind decisions, concepts, and architecture. Explanation illuminates and provides perspective.

## What Belongs Here

### ✅ Include:
- **Architectural decisions** - Why we chose approach X over Y
- **Conceptual overviews** - The big picture and how pieces fit together
- **Design philosophy** - Principles guiding the framework
- **Trade-offs and alternatives** - Different approaches and their implications
- **Historical context** - Why things evolved this way
- **Theory and background** - Academic or technical foundations
- **Opinions and perspectives** - When grounded in expertise
- **Connections between concepts** - How ideas relate

### ❌ Don't Include:
- Step-by-step instructions (save for [Tutorials](../tutorials/) or [How-to Guides](../how-to-guides/))
- Complete API specifications (save for [Reference](../reference/))
- Procedural task directions (save for [How-to Guides](../how-to-guides/))

## Writing Guidelines

1. **Provide context** - Help readers understand the landscape
2. **Make connections** - Link concepts together
3. **Discuss alternatives** - Show what else was considered
4. **Take perspectives** - It's okay to have opinions here
5. **Use analogies** - Help readers build mental models
6. **Circle around topics** - Approach from different angles
7. **Link generously** - Connect to tutorials, how-tos, and reference

## Example Explanation Topics

- Understanding OAuth in the AT Protocol
- Why We Use Iron Session for Cookie Management
- The Architecture of Collective Social
- AT Protocol vs. ActivityPub: Key Differences
- Understanding the Firehose and Real-time Data
- Security Considerations in Decentralized Social Apps
- The Role of DIDs in Identity Management
- Design Principles Behind the Framework

## Explanation Structure Template

```markdown
# Understanding [Topic]

Brief introduction to what will be explained.

## Context
Where does this fit in the bigger picture?

## The Problem/Question
What problem does this solve or question does this answer?

## Background
Historical context, related concepts, foundational knowledge.

## How It Works
Conceptual explanation (not step-by-step instructions).

## Why This Approach
Reasoning behind design decisions.

## Alternatives Considered
What else was possible and why this was chosen.

## Trade-offs
Benefits and limitations of this approach.

## Implications
What this means for users/developers.

## See Also
- Link to related explanations
- Link to reference for technical details
- Link to tutorials that use this concept
```
